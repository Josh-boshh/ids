FAQ
Frequently asked questions
How long does a typical project take?
It depends on scope — a straightforward corporate site can launch in a few weeks, while a multi-section platform with registration systems or live data takes longer. We give you a firm timeline as part of the proposal.
How soon can we start?
Once scope and budget are agreed, we can typically begin within a couple of weeks, depending on our current project load.
Do you offer ongoing support after launch, or is it a one-off build?
Both — every project ships with a defined post-launch support window, and we also work on a retainer basis for clients who need continuous updates.
Can we see more of your work before committing?
Yes — the case studies on this page link through to live sites we've built, including a federal ministry, a political accountability portal, and a state-government commodity platform.
Can you handle compliance-heavy or highly regulated projects?
Yes — that's a lot of our recent work. Our Federal Ministry of Defence rebuild, for example, was delivered to meet Bureau of Public Service Reforms (BPSR) compliance criteria for federal government websites.
