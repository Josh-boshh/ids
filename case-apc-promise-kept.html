APC Promise Kept

A publicly accessible accountability portal built for the All Progressives Congress to document President Bola Ahmed Tinubu's Renewed Hope Agenda.

Visit live site

The brief

Overview

The site serves as a structured record of fulfilled campaign promises — with individual pages per commitment, live news updates, APC Chairman profile, and impact milestone counters showing 9.1M+ Nigerians reached, ₦200B in MSME support, and 17,600 healthcare centres activated.

The design prioritises credibility over promotion — clean, institutional, and mobile-optimised for Nigeria's smartphone-heavy audience. Full SEO and social meta tags (Open Graph, Twitter Card) were implemented so every share generates a rich preview across WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram.

The proof

Key deliverables

  • Commitments database with individual detail pages per promise
  • Homepage impact counters and milestone display
  • APC Chairman profile and photo gallery
  • News feed and APC Radio section
  • Full SEO, social meta tags, and mobile-first responsive design
